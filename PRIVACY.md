# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Encounter Daily ("we", "our", "us") is a daily devotional reading app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

- **Account Information**: When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive and store your email address and display name (and your provider account identifier) to create and authenticate your account.
- **Reading Data**: We store your daily reading completion status, journal notes, and app preferences (such as preferred Bible translation and font size).
- **Bookmarks & Search History**: We store the passages you bookmark and your in-app Bible search history to personalize your experience.
- **Device & Analytics Data**: We use Google Firebase Analytics, which collects device identifiers (including the advertising ID for Android 12 and earlier), app usage data, and crash reports to monitor performance and improve the app. Our servers also log IP addresses and basic request data for security and troubleshooting.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide daily reading content and track your reading progress
- To save and sync your journal notes and bookmarks
- To remember your app preferences
- To analyze usage and diagnose errors so we can improve the app
- To protect the security and integrity of the service

## AI Summarization

When you use the "Summarize" feature on your journal notes, the notes you submit are sent to DeepSeek (a third-party AI service) solely to generate the summary. We do not use your notes to train or market to you, and they are processed only to fulfill your summarization request.

## Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers and transmitted over HTTPS. Your journal notes, bookmarks, and reading progress are private to your account and are not sold or rented to any third party.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google Sign-In and Facebook Login**: Used for authentication only. We receive your name and email address from these providers.
- **Firebase Analytics**: Used for app analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring.
- **DeepSeek**: Used only to generate AI summaries of your journal notes when you request them.

## Data Deletion

You may delete your account and associated data (profile information, reading history, journal notes, bookmarks, and preferences) at any time. See our account deletion page:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jkanimea/dailyreadingApp/main/DELETE_ACCOUNT.md

Or email us at the address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is intended for users aged 6 and older, and we do not target advertising to children.
